Stabilo

Select text on a web page and click the marker icon. Select highlighted text again to erase it, or click a highlight to edit its note.

Keyboard: select text, then Tab and Enter to highlight or erase. Alt+Shift+H also works. Alt+Shift+N opens the next highlight’s note. Escape closes the editor.

Works automatically on ordinary websites with site access allowed. PDFs and browser pages aren’t supported.

Highlights and notes disappear when you reload or leave this page.
